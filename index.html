About me

Growing up in Belize, I noticed from a young age how technology would shape the future of our country.

As a small nation, we hold a unique edge: fewer people means the opportunity for tighter tech literacy. Since that realization, I've devoted myself to showing people what's possible.

I studied computer information systems at St. John's College Junior College and information technology at the University of Belize, and I've been building websites for local businesses since 2017. By day I work as a database developer, after years in tech support and integrations.

In 2017 I also started HipHop Belize, a platform for urban Belizean music. At its peak it pulled millions of impressions, and it's how a lot of young Belizeans first heard my name. I still post on it today, on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

Belize Bus Schedules started when I tried to rebuild the Department of Transport's old website and realized there was no single place to check when a bus leaves. Today more than 17,000 people use it every month, and I still track down and update every schedule myself.

The same idea runs through everything I make, from a reading game that helps people practise reading aloud to MayaExplorer, which will bring virtual tours and quizzes for our Maya sites to anyone with a phone. If something can make life in Belize a little easier, I want to build it.

Edge-first, built on Cloudflare

I like building edge-first because it keeps things simple and quick to work with. For a country Belize's size, even a popular app is unlikely to have more than around 100,000 people on it at once, and Cloudflare handles that comfortably. Instead of looking after servers myself, I can spend that time on the app. Belize Bus Schedules runs on Workers and D1 and serves 17K+ people a month, and If Yo Cya Read is hosted on Pages.
